Community Standards

Rules

Everyone in the group is expected to follow these. Read them before you play, and again if you're ever unsure where a line is.

Sub-Section 1.1

Player Guidelines & Rights

Section 1.11 Player Guidelines

  • Roblox's Terms of Service and our own in-game rules apply to everyone, all the time, no exceptions for personal opinion. Breaking either one puts your standing at risk: expect at least an unwave from the community, and it can go as far as an indefinite ban.
  • If you see something that breaks the rules, say something. A community that reports on itself stays fair and enjoyable for everyone, and the mod team can only step in fast when players flag issues as they happen.
  • Exploiting gets zero warnings. Get caught, and you're removed from the community for good.

Section 1.12 Rights

  • Getting punished isn't the end of the conversation. Anyone can appeal through the proper channel, and the moderation team will look at the full picture before making a final call.
  • That right isn't a loophole. Flooding the system with appeals, twisting the facts, or trying to game the process won't lighten a punishment; it'll make the next one harsher.
Sub-Section 1.2

Spawn Rules & Glitching

Section 1.21 Spawn Killing/Camping

  • Attacking or killing someone still inside their spawn is off-limits, full stop, until they've put 10-15 studs between themselves and it. Once they've cleared that distance, it's fair game. Break this and expect an unwave.
  • Camping a spawn so tightly that someone can't even step out without dying counts the same way as spawnkilling. The whole map is there to fight on; use it instead of parking outside someone's exit.
  • Forcing or glitching a player out of their own spawn just to get an easy kill falls under this same rule.

Section 1.22 Glitching

  • Bugs and glitches aren't free shortcuts. Using one for extra speed, extra damage, or any other edge (the swim-animation speed trick is a common example) earns an in-game ban, and possibly an unwave on top of it. M1 + dash is fine for now and isn't treated as exploiting.
  • Clipping through walls, gates, or any boundary you're not meant to cross is a different level of serious: it's an automatic, permanent ban. The map's edges exist for a reason; stay inside them.
Sub-Section 1.3

Conduct & Fair Play

Section 1.31 Community Conduct

  • Harassment, hate speech, and discrimination toward any member aren't tolerated in any form, whether that's in-game, in voice, or in Discord.
  • Making an alternate account to dodge a ban or mute counts as evading punishment. If it's caught, both accounts lose their access.
  • Impersonating a staff member, in-game or in chat, is treated as seriously as the authority you're pretending to have.

Section 1.32 Fair Play

  • Advertising other servers, groups, or Discords in our spaces isn't allowed without staff permission first.
  • A staff member's call stands at the moment it's made. If you disagree with one, use the appeal process in Section 1.12 instead of arguing it out on the spot.
