Community Standards

Rules

Everyone in the group is expected to follow these. Read them before you play, and again if you're ever unsure where a line is.

Sub-Section 1.1

Player Guidelines & Rights

Section 1.11 Player Guidelines

  • All players must follow the Roblox Terms of Service and all in-game rules at all times, regardless of personal opinion. Players who fail to comply with these standards or violate the Roblox ToS will receive an unwave from the community and may be banned indefinitely.
  • All players are required to report any actions that violate the Roblox Terms of Service or our in-game rules. Reporting rule violations helps the moderation team address issues quickly and keeps the game fair, safe, and enjoyable for the whole community.
  • Exploiting is strictly prohibited and will result in permanent removal from the community.

Section 1.12 Rights

  • Everyone has the right to appeal any punishment they receive. Appeals can be submitted through the proper channels and will be reviewed by the moderation team, who will consider the full situation before making a final decision.
  • Abusing the appeals system or attempting to exploit your rights in any way will not be tolerated. Any attempt to manipulate, misrepresent, or repeatedly challenge a punishment without valid reason will result in stricter disciplinary action.
Sub-Section 1.2

Spawn Rules & Glitching

Section 1.21 Spawn Killing/Camping

  • Spawnkilling is strictly prohibited. Players may not attack, shoot, or kill others while they're in their spawn area, except once the target has moved 10-15 studs away from spawn, at which point it's no longer considered spawnkilling. Spawnkilling results in an unwave.
  • Spawn camping is strictly prohibited: players may not camp someone's spawn to the point where they can't leave without being killed. Let them out of their spawn and use the whole map for combat.
  • Players may not use glitches or exploits to pull other players out of their spawn in order to kill them.

Section 1.22 Glitching

  • Players caught exploiting glitches or bugs to gain an unfair advantage in speed, damage, or any other aspect of gameplay will not be tolerated (e.g. abusing the swimming animation for an unfair speed boost). Failure to uphold this rule will result in an in-game ban and a possible unwave. (M1 + dash is allowed for now.)
  • Players caught exploiting glitches to pass through gates, walls, or other boundaries will be permanently banned from the game. All players are expected to respect the environment and play within the intended boundaries.
